    About

    AniQuiz Insights is a non-commercial portfolio project. It combines a trivia quiz with a personal analytics dashboard, applying item-analysis ideas from test design and quality control.

    All questions are original text written for this project. Anime titles, character names and studio names belong to their respective owners; no official images, audio or synopses are hosted here. This site is not affiliated with any studio, publisher, AniList or MyAnimeList.

    Data stays in your browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent to a server.

    Takedown or correction requests: please open an issue on the project repository.
